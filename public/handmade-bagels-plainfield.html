The bagelry · Kim’s counter

Handmade bagels, with cream cheese

The bagels at Plainfield Bakehouse come from Blue Heron Bagels — the business Kim Whisenhunt launched in 2024, built on making bagels by hand and selling out fast, often within half an hour of opening the orders.

Home kitchens have a ceiling, and bagels hit it first: you can’t do cream cheese the way it should be done out of a home kitchen. Kim was already looking at a commercial kitchen when this storefront came up — a historic downtown space that has housed bakeries for years, a block from the square, in a town that is part of both owners’ stories.

“It just didn’t make sense to put time and money into equipment or building out the home bakery… Someone brought this up to me, and I was like, ‘You know what, maybe I want to switch altogether.’”

Kim Whisenhunt, as told to Patch (November 2026)

At Plainfield Bakehouse, the bagelry gets a real kitchen of its own, with its own baking staff, and cream cheese finally on the other side of the bagel. Everything else on this site lives a few steps from the bookshelves — which is exactly the point.

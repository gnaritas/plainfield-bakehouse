Plainfield Bakehouse is a handmade bagelry, sourdough bakehouse and used bookshop from two Plainfield bakers — Kim Whisenhunt of Blue Heron Bagels and Michaela Gallaher of Gallaher Sourdough — taking over a historic Lockport Street storefront that has housed bakeries for years.

One storefront, two bakeries, a bookshop, and a rule: no tips, no gluten-free and no drama.

In plain words, from the owners

No tips. No gluten-free. No drama.

No tips

We pay our employees a fair wage so nobody depends on inconsistent tips. If you’d still like to tip us, donate to the Plainfield Food Pantry — or give us an honest Google review. That helps more than money.

No gluten-free, on purpose

We love the gluten-free community too much to bake something potentially hazardous for them, and it isn’t legal to produce gluten-free goods in a kitchen full of gluten. Ask at the counter and we’ll refer you to gluten-free bakeries in the area.

No drama

Two friends, one shared kitchen, separate bakers, one storefront that has been a bakery for years. Everyone gets a good morning — that’s the whole plan.

Our story

Two home bakeries walked into one storefront.

Michaela Gallaher has baked sourdough under Gallaher Sourdough since 2023; Kim Whisenhunt launched Blue Heron Bagels in 2024. Both kept selling out — sometimes within thirty minutes — and both needed a commercial kitchen. A shared one turned out to come with a downtown bakery legacy.

Straight from the owners’ plans

What’s coming

Here’s what the shop is being built around. Specifics and prices will post at the counter when the doors open — this is the concept, in the owners’ own words as told to local press.

Handmade bagels, with cream cheese

Kim Whisenhunt bakes the bagelry side — the Blue Heron Bagels line that sold out weekend after weekend, moved from a home kitchen to the shop’s commercial kitchen, where cream cheese is finally on the table.

Sourdough breads, sweets & pastries

Michaela Gallaher bakes the bakehouse side — sourdough loaves plus sweets and pastries, the same bakes that grew Gallaher Sourdough since 2023.

A custom morning blend from Krema

Coffee isn’t an afterthought downtown: Plainfield Bakehouse partnered with neighboring Krema Coffee House to craft a morning blend made for the bakery. No rivalry — a bagel in one hand, good coffee from a neighbor in the other.

A wall of used books

Well-loved books at cheaper-than-new prices, yours to keep — and yes, you can stay and read while you have a bagel with cream cheese or a coffee.

Also in the shop

Branded merchandise, nostalgic candy, and an art vending machine featuring custom prints by local artist Nicole Eimer of Fresh Prints.

Not baking gluten-free, on purpose — see the policy. Seating for roughly 10–12 guests. Planned hours Tuesday–Sunday, 7 a.m.–1 p.m.

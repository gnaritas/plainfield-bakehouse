Privacy

The contact form. When you send a note, the name, phone, email and message you type are delivered to the shop’s inbox so the owners can reply. Nothing is sold, and the information is used for that reply and nothing else.

No cookies, no trackers, no ad pixels. This site sets no first-party or third-party cookies, runs no analytics scripts and loads no advertising code. There is nothing to consent to while you browse.

The map. The address view on the home and contact pages embeds Google Maps directly (no tracking parameters on the embed). When the map loads, Google processes your IP address as part of serving the map, under Google’s own privacy policy.

Hosting logs. Like any website, basic server records (page requested, approximate time, browser type) are kept for a short time to keep the site running.

Questions. Email info@plainfieldbakehouse.com and a person will read it. This page covers this website only.

The bakehouse · Michaela’s counter

Sourdough breads, sweets & pastries

Michaela Gallaher has been baking sourdough as Gallaher Sourdough since 2023 — a cottage bakery run full-time out of Plainfield, recognized with a “Food & Bev Business of the Year 2025” badge from @yourpsacc and a following that could clear a batch in thirty minutes.

“I was against the idea of a storefront forever. It was only this summer, when my demand got unbearable. It became no longer fun because everyone was upset they couldn’t get bread… Because of that, I was more open to the idea.”

Michaela Gallaher, as told to Patch (November 2026)

The Bakehouse gives that demand a real kitchen: sourdough loaves, sweets and pastries, baked with her own staff on her own side of the shared space — the same doughs, now with room to grow.

And one thing we won’t bake

No gluten-free, and that’s a promise, not a gap. It isn’t legal to produce gluten-free goods in a kitchen full of gluten, and the owners won’t risk a loaf that could be hazardous for the people who need it. Ask at the counter and you’ll get a recommendation for a gluten-free bakery in the area that does it right.

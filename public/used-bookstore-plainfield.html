The bookshop · the front of the shop

A wall of used books

Downtown Plainfield doesn’t currently have a shop that gives well-loved books a second life. Plainfield Bakehouse is putting one in the front of the store — a wall of used books at cheaper-than-new prices, and they’re yours once you buy them.

“When I think about what’s super welcoming in addition to just a bakery, [it’s] a wall of books that you can buy that’s cheaper than buying it new, but you also get to own it… You can stay and read while you have a bagel with cream cheese or coffee.”

Michaela Gallaher, as told to Patch (November 2026)

The bookshop was Michaela’s brainchild — the part of the plan that turns a bakery run into a morning habit. Pick a book, buy it, keep it, or stay a while with it at one of the ten-odd seats. A coffee from the Krema-crafted blend helps.

Books here share the shop with everything else on the menu: bagels, sourdough, sweets, nostalgic candy, merch, and an art vending machine with custom prints by local artist Nicole Eimer of Fresh Prints.
